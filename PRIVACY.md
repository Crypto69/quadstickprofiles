# Privacy policy for QuadStick Profile Studio

Last updated: 23 November 2026

QuadStick Profile Studio does not collect, store or send any personal information.

**What the app keeps, and where.** The profiles you import, your settings and the
backups you take of the QuadStick's drive are saved on your own computer, in the
app's data folder. Files you export are saved in a folder on your computer. Nothing
is uploaded.

**No account.** The app has no sign-in and asks for no name, email address or other
details.

**No internet use.** The app does not need an internet connection and does not
contact any server. It has no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The
app talks only to itself, on your own computer.

**The QuadStick.** The app reads the QuadStick's flash drive to list and back up the
files on it, and the tester reads what the QuadStick reports over USB so it can show
it on screen. Both stay on your computer. The app never changes anything on the
QuadStick.

**Removing your data.** Deleting the app's data folder removes everything it has
saved.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same
address with a new date.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy, or anything else about the app: open an
issue at https://github.com/Crypto69/quadstickprofiles/issues

QuadStick Profile Studio is an independent project and is not affiliated with
QuadStick.
